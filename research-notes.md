## Company profile

Primary source: [Ninewells LinkedIn company page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ninewells/)

- Public name: **NINEWELLS LAW PRACTICE**
- Positioning line: **Experience. Innovation. Trust**
- Industry: Legal Services
- Company size shown by LinkedIn: 11–50 employees
- Headquarters: Victoria Island (Adeyemo Alakija), Lagos
- Type: Privately held
- Public address: Construction House, 18 Adeyemo Alakija, Victoria Island, Lagos 101241, Nigeria
- Publicly listed specialisms:
  - Energy, Extractives & Foreign Investments
  - Dispute (Litigation & Arbitration)
  - Financial Services & Capital Market
  - Corporate Commercial
  - Telecommunication, Technology & Digital Law
- Public about-page themes: a fully integrated multidisciplinary Nigerian firm; commercially astute and precise advice; understanding of clients’ competitive environments, regulatory obligations and risk appetite; collaborative practices; innovation and technology paired with professional values and service excellence.
- The public page did **not** expose an official website, email address or phone number. The prototype therefore links contact calls-to-action to the company’s LinkedIn page rather than inventing a contact channel.

## Publicly announced people

### Prof. Bayo Adaralegbe — Lead Partner

- Public reporting identifies him as Ninewells’ Lead Partner and an energy, arbitration and international-investment lawyer.
- The PUNCH reported that he assumed the role in November 2025 after more than 26 years at Babalakin & Co, where he was a Partner and headed Energy and Extractives.
- He was approved for elevation to Senior Advocate of Nigeria in July 2026, but the reported swearing-in date is 12 October 2026. The prototype deliberately does not use the SAN post-nominal before conferment.
- Sources: [Ninewells LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ninewells/), [The PUNCH — LPPC approval](https://punchng.com/lppc-approves-adaralegbe-mouka-obiaraeri-65-others-for-san-rank/), [The PUNCH — University of Ibadan appointment](https://punchng.com/ui-appoints-lawyer-as-adjunct-professor/)

### Adejoke Odocha — Partner, Corporate & Commercial

- Ninewells announced her arrival on 3 August 2026.
- The announcement described previous experience as General Counsel at Rendeavour West Africa, legal-department roles at Schlumberger and Atlantic Energy, and earlier private practice at Templars.
- Publicly announced capabilities include regulatory compliance, commercial negotiation and drafting, loan documentation and perfection, employment, real estate and strategic legal advice.
- Sources: [Ninewells LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ninewells/), [Adejoke Odocha’s public LinkedIn profile](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/adejoke-odocha-0212ab2)

### Abdulmajeed Abolaji — Managing Associate, Energy, Extractives & Foreign Investment

- Ninewells announced his arrival on 29 July 2026.
- The announcement described prior work at Dentons ACAS-Law across its Energy and Financial Services practices, with experience in oil, gas, power and financial-services transactions.
- On 31 July 2026, Ninewells highlighted his UpgradeTV Energy Outlook appearance covering Nigeria’s production recovery, investor interest, energy reforms, upstream divestments and host-community participation.
- Sources: [Ninewells LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ninewells/), [Abdulmajeed Abolaji’s public LinkedIn profile](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/abdulmajeed-abolaji-a51974162)

### Esther Gbenro — Senior Associate, Telecommunications, Technology & Digital Law

- Ninewells announced her arrival on 28 July 2026 and identified Aluko & Oyebode as her previous firm.
- Her public profile says she heads Ninewells’ Telecommunications, Technology & Digital Law practice.
- Her profile records first-class honours from Nigerian Law School and WIPO certifications in copyright, patent information and intellectual property.
- Her recent public analysis considers how the CBN’s data-localisation requirement could affect Nigerian data-centre investment, while distinguishing storage location from data security and governance.
- Sources: [Ninewells LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ninewells/), [Esther Gbenro’s public LinkedIn profile](https://ng.linkedin.com/in/esther-ajayi-gbenro)

### Adebimpe Fajemisin — Chief Operating Officer

- Ninewells announced her arrival on 30 July 2026.
- The announcement referenced prior business-support experience at Olaniwun Ajayi and Templars, and capability in operations, strategic management, organisational behaviour and law-firm business planning.
- Source: [Ninewells LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ninewells/)
